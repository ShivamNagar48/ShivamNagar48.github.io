Privacy Policy

Shop Empire: Store Tycoon (Android) · Effective 2026-11-14

This policy explains what information Shop Empire: Store Tycoon ("the game", "we") and the services it uses handle, and the choices you have. The game is published by Shivam Nagar.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your progress (your shop, money, level, settings and the items you bought) is saved on your phone only. We have no account system and no server that stores your game.
  • Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this progress. One-time purchases can be restored from Google Play with Settings → Restore purchases.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch, and short ads between play (removed by the "No ads" purchase).
  • AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show, measure and (where you allow it) personalize ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Crash reports and settings (Google Firebase)

  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the game crashes or hits an error: the device model, Android version, the game's version, the error's details and a random installation ID. We use it only to fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config lets us adjust game numbers (for example how often ads appear) without an update. It uses a random installation ID and receives no personal information.
  • Firebase Analytics (from version 1.0.1) records how the game is played, for example finishing the tutorial, reaching a level, ending a day, watching a reward video or opening the store, with a random app-instance ID and general device information (model, Android version, country). We use it only to improve the game. It is switched on only if you agree in the consent form (where the law requires asking) or where no consent is needed; it never collects your advertising ID. If you refuse consent, no analytics data is collected.
  • Firebase's privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases (Google Play)

  • In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game receives only what you bought and a purchase token, to give you the item.
  • Google Play's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules a few reminders on your phone (for example when your shop needs restocking). They are created on your device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in Settings or in Android's settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or microphone.
  • We don't sell your information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Keeping and deleting data

Crash reports are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days. Advertising data is kept according to Google's policies. Because we hold no account for you, uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device; to ask about data held by the services above, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
